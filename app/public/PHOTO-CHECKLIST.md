# Fenster Job Photo Checklist

Date started: 2026-07-16
Why: the website's biggest content gap is real photos of our own installs. The image audit (see `ACTION-PLAN.md` and `PROGRESS.md`) removed stock photos, CGI renders and wrong-product images; supplier photography is filling the gap short term, but photos of Fenster jobs are the only durable fix. Five phone photos per job is enough.

## The 5-shot checklist (per completed job)

1. **Straight-on exterior** — stand square to the wall, whole product in frame, no vans/tools/bins in shot.
2. **Interior view** — the product from inside the room, curtains/blinds open, lights off if bright.
3. **Detail close-up** — handle, lock, meeting rail, threshold or glazing bar. One arm's length away.
4. **Open position** — sash lifted, casement open, bifold folded back, slider open.
5. **Street/context shot** — the product on the house from the pavement or drive (this powers the town pages).

## Rules that make photos usable

- Landscape orientation, phone held level.
- Daylight, not dusk. Avoid shooting into the sun; put the sun behind or beside you.
- Wipe the glass and remove protective tape/labels first.
- No people, faces, cars with readable plates, or house numbers close-up (blur-worthy details cost editing time).
- If the customer is around, ask: "Do you mind if we take a few photos of the finished work for our website?" Verbal yes is fine; note the first name and town.

## Naming and handover

- Send the same day to the office (WhatsApp group or email to info@) with: **product type, colour/finish, town** (e.g. "Flush casement, Anthracite, Bletchley").
- Office saves to the shared job-photos folder under `YYYY-MM product town/`.
- The website pass picks the best shots quarterly; a good street shot from each of the 13 matrix towns is a standing wishlist.
