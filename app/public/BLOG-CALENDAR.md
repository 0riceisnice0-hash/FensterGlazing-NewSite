Weekly Monday release. Straight-and-helpful tone. All 52 posts are **written, validated and scheduled** in `inc/blog-posts.php`; each releases itself on its date with no manual step.

## How the system works

- Posts are data in `inc/blog-posts.php` with a `publish_date`; the route, the `/blog/` hub card and the sitemap entry appear automatically on that date. Until then the URL 404s.
- Rendering uses the dedicated `blog-post.php` template: product hero pattern (eyebrow with date, H1, lead, green-then-steel CTA pair, one real product photo), article-measure body with one or two captioned feature images from the declared products' pools, the post's own routes panel, then the shared enquiry form with explicit contrast.
- Copy rules enforced by `validate.php` (scratch tooling, rerun on any edit): weekly Monday dates, metas 70-160 chars, headings end with a full stop or question mark, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no third-person Fenster voice, products must exist in the media pools, and sash windows are only ever "A rated".
- No prices, lead times or performance figures unless already verified on the matching product page. Timescales are always "confirmed at order".
